MyChoice

How do you get the Priceless Gift of Eternity?

It is simple: accept God's love. God loves you and me with everlasting love. To accept God's love, say this simple prayer audibly:

“Lord, I confess with my mouth that Jesus is Lord, and I believe in my heart that God raised him from the dead. Amen.”

If you sincerely said the above prayer, you are now a child of God and have been freely given the priceless gift of eternity. Read God’s word in the Bible and follow what it says. It might be good to join a church based on God’s uncompromising word.

“If you openly declare that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved. For it is by believing in your heart that you are made right with God, and it is by openly declaring your faith that you are saved.”
(Romans 10:9–10 NLT)
