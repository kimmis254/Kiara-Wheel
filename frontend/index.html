Introduction

This book is for anyone facing unexpected life challenges. It shares a personal journey of overcoming a life-threatening disease through the discovery of The 7 Priceless Gifts.

Written over five years and spanning multiple countries, this book offers a unique framework for building a mindset to overcome adversity, God's way. You'll explore practical wisdom, emotional strength, and actionable steps—from building your "Go-To Team" to understanding superfoods and unconditional love.

Ultimately, this book aims to inspire you to take action, bringing you closer to God, family, and your true purpose. Join this journey of healing and restoration and discover how you too can overcome the unexpected.

About the Author

Francis Njiraini is a child of God, a loving husband, and a caring father, deeply passionate about **Business & Families**. As a Tech Executive at a leading global technology consulting firm, with a background as a software engineer and certified accountant, Francis is dedicated to provoking growth—both in business and in families—through innovative use of technology and nutrition.

His core mission in life is to **connect families through Nutrition**. Beyond his professional pursuits, Francis enjoys playing golf, traveling, and reading. He currently resides in Nairobi, Kenya, with his beloved wife, Kuhi Njiraini, and their three children: Renee, Angelina, and Ryan.
