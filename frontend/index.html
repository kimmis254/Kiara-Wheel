One Christian Man's Journey To Beat Cancer

The 7 Priceless Gifts™ That Led Me to Remission

What if the deadliest diagnosis of your life became the greatest gift?

When Francis Njiraini—a devoted husband, father, and successful executive in Nairobi—was suddenly diagnosed with aggressive cancer, everything stopped. The future he had planned for his young family felt uncertain, and the question “Why me?” echoed in the quiet hours.

Yet in the midst of fear, treatment far from home, and the raw uncertainty of survival, something unexpected unfolded. Through unwavering faith, the fierce love of his wife and children, and moments of profound clarity, Francis discovered what he now calls the 7 Priceless Gifts™—truths about divine guidance, unconditional love, purpose, resilience, and eternity, revealed not in spite of cancer, but because of it.

One Christian Man’s Journey to Beat Cancer is a deeply personal memoir of walking through the valley with honesty and hope. It is the story of a man who refused to let a devastating diagnosis define his life—and who emerged five years in remission with a renewed calling to inspire fathers, families, and anyone facing an uncertain road.

This is not a medical manual or a promise of easy answers. It is a testimony of grace in the fire, reminding us that even the hardest journeys can lead to deeper faith, stronger love, and a life of lasting purpose.

BOOK OVERVIEW

When Francis Njiraini—a devoted husband, father, and successful executive—was diagnosed with aggressive cancer, his world stopped.

The plans he had carefully built.
The future he imagined for his family.
The sense of control he had always known.

All of it vanished in a single moment.

What followed was not just a medical battle, but a deeply personal journey through fear, faith, surrender, and transformation.

One Christian Man’s Journey to Beat Cancer is not a clinical guide or a collection of medical advice. It is the true story of a man who stood face-to-face with mortality—and discovered that the most powerful healing often begins on the inside.

As treatments began and uncertainty grew, Francis was forced to confront questions many of us avoid.

  • What really matters when everything else falls away?
  • How do you lead your family when you’re afraid?
  • What does faith look like when the outcome is unknown?
  • Can purpose exist even in suffering?

Through prayer, reflection, and the unwavering love of his family, Francis began to see his journey not as a punishment—but as an invitation.

An invitation to grow.
To surrender.
To trust.
And to receive what he now calls the 7 Priceless Gifts™.

Inside these pages, you’ll discover:

  • How crisis can clarify what truly matters most
  • Why faith isn’t about certainty—but about courage
  • The quiet power of family, presence, and perseverance
  • How to find meaning even when the path feels unfair
  • What it means to live fully, even when the future feels fragile

This is not a story of denial or false positivity.
It is a story of honesty, endurance, and grace in the face of the unknown.

Whether you are facing illness yourself, supporting someone you love, or simply searching for hope during a difficult season, this book offers companionship and perspective.

It reminds us that healing doesn’t always mean a cure—and that some of life’s most profound gifts arrive disguised as trials.

If you or someone you love is walking through uncertainty, this book is a reminder that you are not alone, you are not without hope, and your story is far from over.

❝Recap:
A powerful and thought-provoking story of Francis Njiraini, who shares his journey to overcome cancer using 7 priceless gifts. Francis weaves the challenging journey of diagnosis, treatment, healing, and restoration with sneak peeks of his life with family, and friends and walk with God. He also weaves in thought-provoking nuggets and analogies to explain the different priceless gifts. This beautiful weaving provides breathers for an otherwise intense topic of cancer.

I have read this book within 24 hours, on the road and at every opportunity I could get at work. I got hooked especially from the Priceless Gift chapters, only to realize that I am on page 114 in no time. I didn’t want the book to end. I kept writing notes and pausing to think.

I have many favorite parts of the book:

My first is Francis’ wife. Her calmness, especially after such unexpected heavy news, was very captivating and inspiring. When Francis said they both realized that they were at peace despite the life-changing news, I had questions. What’s the foundation for such calmness in the face of such news? But these questions were sufficiently answered as I read on.

France’s story is one of my favorites. There were serious things to talk about and tough decisions to make because as a reader I was already invested. But the narration on the France experience, especially the Monaco adventure got me soaked in. I was smiling all through and I completely forgot about the diagnosis and the journey ahead. The description of your travel experiences is excellent. You immersed me in the experiences. I was smiling and enjoying my mental pictures as if I was there. Thank you for sharing your experiences so vividly.

The book is also very informative. Incredibly informative. From the history of different places to nutrition. I took notes and found myself wanting to know more about superfoods. Francis shared simplified and valuable information on nutrition.

The Divine Helpers chapter, Project Victory decision and reuniting with the kids are some of the scenes that made me very emotional. Reading how your family and friends came together to make your kids as comfortable as possible was emotional in a powerful way. It was a beautiful testament to the role the divine helpers played in your journey.

I found myself thinking about my GT Team. I ended up deciding to talk to one of them about a tough career decision I have been trying to make alone. The book provides actionable tools that I’m keeping with me.

Some More Feedback Notes:

Introduction: The introduction does a great job distinguishing precious and priceless, but I found myself wanting to jump some parts of the analogies to get to the next chapter. And on a lighter note, I was also curious to know your wife’s hairstyle when you were traveling to India.

Overall, I have enjoyed reading the book. I have learned. I have been inspired, encouraged and challenged by the 7 gifts. The gifts stand out for me because they are relevant and applicable to my life. I would recommend the book to all my friends and family. Whether they are going through any unexpected situations or not.❞

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Francis Njiraini is a husband, father of three, and a technology executive. Now five years in remission, he lives in Nairobi with his wife Kuhi and their children—Renee, Angelina, and Ryan. Through his story, Francis seeks to encourage others, especially fathers, to face adversity with faith, courage, and the priceless gifts no diagnosis can take away.

A Special Note to You, Our Reader

Thank you for following along on my journey. As part of writing this book, I hope to create enough of a connection with you to encourage you on your own journey. Whether you are facing an unexpected situation or not, I pray that this book inspires you to take some actions and may these actions impact your life.

Could you please do me a quick favor?

As a family we faced one of our greatest crises in our life, when I got the diagnosis. Our faith, our emotions and our finances were stretched beyond words. This diagnosis threatened my health, my family, my work, my identity, my dreams and my future. I have poured my heart and soul into this project and spent hundreds of hours writing this book. I have edited the book seven times with plenty of help and there have been a lot of other behind the scenes tasks. This has been a massive project that has taken me five years to complete.
