Introduction

This book is for you, who was having a good life then the unexpected happened, you faced or are facing a life changing battle that has become an obstacle in your life. God willing, it is possible to overcome.

I thought I was living a good life, and then the unexpected happened and had to face a life-threatening disease. I walked into my doctor's room healthy, or so I thought and came out a few minutes later with a label. I had just been diagnosed with Cancer. Throughout my treatment and recovery journey, I came face to face with real hard questions about my life, and with God's help, this led to the discovery of the 7 Priceless Gifts™ framework that helped me in my journey.

Every person’s journey is different. Your unexpected situation may be different. It may not be a diagnosis but it may be a career choice that you need to make or an obstacle on your path. I thought sharing my story with you would increase your chance of overcoming the unexpected situation you might be facing now or in the future.

I have written this book for over five years in different locations; Nairobi, Johannesburg, Dar Es Salaam, Mauritius, Kigali, Vienna, Kampala & Madrid, and I am in my 5th year of remission (2025) as I complete this book. The book explores how the 7 Priceless gifts™ can transform one’s mindset when confronted with adversity, offering deep inspiration, comfort, and encouragement during difficult times. It provides practical wisdom and emotional strength, serving as a beacon of light for anyone facing their battles. The practical steps include forming a GT Team (Go To Team), building daily routines, and incorporating superfoods in your meals. The mindset change starts by building hope to live, taking a long term perspective and using the Kiara Wheel tool to create your personalized Kiara Wheel to clarify your “why?”. I hope & pray that the 7 Priceless Gifts™ will inspire you to take action and bring you closer to God, your family and your purpose and may you overcome your unexpected battle.

About the Author
